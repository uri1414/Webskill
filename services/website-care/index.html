Launch is the start, not the finish line

A website isn't a set-it-and-forget-it purchase. Software needs updates, content gets stale, and small issues creep in. Our care plans keep your site fast, secure, backed up, and current, so you never have to think about it.

Beyond maintenance, we help your site keep improving, new pages, fresh content, and small optimizations that compound over time. Think of it as having a design and dev team on call, without hiring one.

What's included

  • Managed hosting, backups, and security
  • Software updates and uptime monitoring
  • A monthly allowance for edits and new pages
  • Ongoing performance and SEO tune-ups
  • Simple monthly reporting

No lock-in, ever

Care plans are month-to-month and optional. You own everything we build, and you're free to leave anytime. Most clients stay because it's genuinely easier to have the people who built the site keep it healthy and growing, not because they're stuck.
