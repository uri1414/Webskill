Cleaning that can pass procurement scrutiny

Public buildings carry a higher bar. They need contractors who are properly licensed and insured, crews who are background-checked, and documentation that holds up to procurement review. Mid-Valley Facility Services is built for exactly that, providing contract cleaning for city, county, and state facilities across Albany and the Mid-Willamette Valley.

From restrooms and common areas to offices and high-traffic entryways, we keep public spaces clean, safe, and presentable on a dependable schedule. As a locally owned company, we bring accountability and a single point of contact rather than a distant call center, while meeting the compliance standards a public contract demands.

A local contractor built for public work

Government facility managers need a cleaner who can clear procurement, show proof of coverage, and deliver consistently once the contract is signed. Mid-Valley combines that compliance readiness with genuine local accountability.
